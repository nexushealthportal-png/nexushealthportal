Handling

Storing what you receive

Lyophilized solids

Shelf-stable and shipped at ambient temperature. No cold chain is required in transit. For long-term storage keep at -20 °C, desiccated and protected from light.

Solutions

Bacteriostatic water and other solutions ship ambient and should be stored at room temperature. Do not freeze.

Consumables

Vials, filters, and other supplies are sterile and individually wrapped where stated. Store dry at room temperature in original packaging.

Reconstituted stock

Once reconstituted, working life is far shorter than sealed lyophilized material. Aliquot and store per your own protocol.

Returns

Unopened, within 30 days

Unopened items in original packaging may be returned within 30 days. Once a seal is broken a reagent cannot be returned, for contamination control. If a lot does not match its certificate we replace it or refund it in full.
