Handling

How to store what arrives

Freeze-dried powders

They keep at room temperature, so they ship without ice. To store them a long time, keep them in a freezer at -20 °C, dry and out of the light.

Solutions

Bacteriostatic water and other liquids ship at room temperature and should stay there. Do not freeze them.

Supplies

Vials, filters, and the rest are sterile and wrapped one by one where we say so. Keep them dry at room temperature in the packaging they came in.

Opened material

Once a seal is broken, material no longer keeps as long as it did sealed. Storage and handling after that point are the responsibility of the receiving laboratory. We do not provide preparation or usage guidance.

Returns

Unopened, within 30 days

Unopened items in their original packaging can come back within 30 days. Once a seal is broken we cannot take it back, because we cannot rule out contamination. If a batch does not match its report, we replace it or refund you in full.
