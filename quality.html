The checks

What we test on every batch

Purity test (HPLC)

Measures how much of the vial is the compound and how much is anything else. This is the purity number printed on the report.

Identity test (mass spec)

Weighs the molecule itself to confirm the compound is the one named on the label, and not something close to it.

Water left in the powder

Water left behind after freeze-drying. Too much and the powder degrades faster, and you get less actual compound than the label says.

Leftover solvents

Checks that nothing used to make the compound is still in the vial.

Look and dissolving

We look at the powder and mix a sample with liquid to confirm it dissolves the way it should.

Report kept on file

Every batch gets its own number tied to its own report, so you can pull the paperwork for the exact vial you hold.

The process

How it gets to your bench

01

Made to a written spec

Made to a written spec, then freeze-dried into a powder that keeps, and sealed away from light and moisture.

02

Sent out for testing

A sample goes to an outside lab to check purity and confirm the compound. We do not grade our own work.

03

Report filed

The signed report is filed under the batch number before the compound goes up for sale.

04

Sealed and shipped

Vials are sealed and shipped in plain packaging you can tell has been opened, and the report is yours on request.

On the certificate

Shown, not claimed
