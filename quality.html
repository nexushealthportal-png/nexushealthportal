The checks

What we run on every lot

Reverse-phase HPLC

Purity quantified against a reference standard with UV detection. This is the headline figure printed on the certificate.

Mass spectrometry

Molecular identity confirmed by mass-to-charge analysis, so the compound is what the label says it is.

Water content

Residual moisture in the lyophilate, which affects both stability and the real mass of compound per vial.

Residual solvents

Screened against defined limits so nothing from the synthesis carries through to the vial.

Appearance & solubility

Visual inspection of the cake and a reconstitution check before the lot is released.

Lot-level archival

Each batch carries a unique lot number tied to its certificate, so you can pull the paperwork for the exact vial.

The process

From synthesis to your bench

01

Produced to specification

Synthesized to a defined specification and lyophilized to a shelf-stable powder, sealed against light and moisture.

02

Independently assayed

A sample goes to an independent analytical laboratory for HPLC purity and LC-MS identity. We do not grade our own work.

03

Certificate archived

The signed report is filed against the lot number before the compound is listed for sale.

04

Sealed and dispatched

Vials are sealed and shipped in plain, tamper-evident packaging, with the certificate available on request.

On the certificate

Documented, not claimed
