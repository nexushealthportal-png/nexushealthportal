01

What can be returned

Any item in its original packaging with the seal intact may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. There is no restocking fee.

Lab supplies such as vials and filters may be returned within the same window provided the outer packaging is unopened.

02

What cannot be returned

Once a seal is broken we cannot accept the item back, because we cannot rule out contamination and we will not resell material whose handling we cannot account for. This applies even if the contents appear untouched.

This is a contamination-control rule, not a commercial one. It is the same reason we do not sell material that has left our supply chain and come back.

03

If a batch does not match its report

If material does not match the test report filed for its batch, we replace it or refund it in full. This is not limited to the 30 day window and it applies whether or not the seal has been broken.

Tell us the batch number printed on the vial and keep the material and its packaging until we have replied.

04

Damaged or missing on arrival

If a parcel arrives damaged, or an item on your packing list is missing, email us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photograph of the packaging as it arrived.

We replace damaged and missing items at our cost, including return shipping where a return is needed.

05

How to start a return

Email support@nexushealthportal.com with your order number and which items you are returning. We reply with a return address and confirm the refund amount before you ship anything.

Do not send anything back before you have heard from us. Returns that arrive unannounced are difficult to match to an order and slow the refund down.

06

Return shipping

Where the return is our error, a wrong item, a damaged parcel, or a batch that does not match its report, we cover return shipping.

Where you have simply changed your mind, return shipping is yours. We recommend a tracked service, because we can only refund what arrives.

07

When the refund appears

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of the return arriving and passing inspection. How quickly it then shows on your statement is up to your bank, typically a further 3 to 10 days.

We email you when the refund is issued.

08

Cancelling an order

Orders can be cancelled for a full refund any time before they ship. Once tracking has been issued the order is on its way and the return process above applies instead.

Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time with no fee and no minimum number of deliveries. Email us and the next delivery will not be charged.
