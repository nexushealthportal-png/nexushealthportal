Lot traceable

Dispatched fast

Sealed and discreet

In-stock items dispatch within 1 business day in plain, tamper-evident packaging, tracked to your facility.

Each incoming lot is sampled and submitted to an independent analytical laboratory. Identity is established by liquid chromatography with mass spectrometry. Purity is quantified by HPLC with UV detection against a reference standard, and the resulting chromatogram is retained. Peptide lots are additionally checked for water content and residual solvents. A lot is released only when identity is confirmed and purity meets or exceeds the figure published on the product page. The signed report for each released lot is archived and available to purchasers on request.
