What we stand on

Three commitments

We do not grade our own work

An outside lab runs the tests. Us publishing our own purity number would not mean anything.

A number is only as good as its batch

Every number we publish is tied to a batch number you can check against the vial in front of you.

Research use, stated plainly

Everything we sell is for research in a lab. We do not sell it for people or animals, and we do not hint otherwise anywhere on this site.

How we work

The short version

01

Made to a written spec

The compound is made to a written spec, then freeze-dried so it keeps.

02

Send it out for testing

An outside lab measures purity, confirms the compound, and on peptide batches also checks leftover water and solvents.

03

Only list what passes

A batch that misses spec does not go up. There is no second-tier catalog.

04

Ship it sealed and traceable

Plain packaging you can tell has been opened, tracked, with the batch number on the vial and the report on file.

Research use only

Nexus Research sells lab chemicals and lab supplies only for research in a lab. Our products are not drugs, foods, cosmetics, or dietary supplements, and nothing on this site constitutes medical advice.
