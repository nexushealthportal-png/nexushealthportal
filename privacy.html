01

What we collect

To fulfil an order we collect your name, email address, receiving institution or company, delivery address, and phone number if you give one.

We also keep a record of what you ordered and when, which is what lets us tie a lot number back to the shipment it went out in.

If you subscribe to catalog updates we keep your email address for that purpose until you unsubscribe.

02

Payment information

Card details are handled by our payment processor and are not stored on our systems. We never see or retain a full card number.

We retain only the transaction reference and the amount, which we need for accounting and for issuing refunds.

03

Why we hold it

To take, fulfil, and ship your order. To answer support requests and issue certificates for the lots you received. To meet accounting and record-keeping obligations.

We do not build advertising profiles and we do not sell, rent, or trade your information to anyone.

04

Who sees it

Our shipping carrier receives the name and address needed to deliver the parcel. Our payment processor receives what it needs to take payment.

We may disclose information where the law requires it. Beyond that, no third party receives your data.

05

Cookies

This site uses local browser storage to remember your cart and your research-use acknowledgement between visits. That data stays in your browser and is not transmitted to us.

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

06

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as accounting and product-traceability obligations require, and then deleted.

Catalog-update subscriptions are deleted as soon as you unsubscribe.

07

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it.

Email support@nexushealthportal.com and we will respond. Every catalog-update email carries an unsubscribe link.

08

Security and changes

The site is served over HTTPS and access to order records is limited to those who need it.

If we change this notice we publish the new version here with a new effective date.
