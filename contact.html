What we cannot help with

Two things we will not answer

01

How to prepare or use a compound

We do not provide preparation, handling, or usage guidance for any product. Everything we sell is a laboratory reagent, and how it is used is the responsibility of the receiving lab.

Anything medical

We are a chemical supplier, not a pharmacy and not a compounding facility. Nothing we say is medical advice, and we decline orders where the stated use is human consumption.
