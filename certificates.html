What is on it

Inside a certificate

Compound and lot number

The exact identifier printed on your vial, so the paperwork and the material cannot be confused.

HPLC purity

The measured purity figure against a reference standard, with the analytical method stated.

LC-MS identity

Confirmation that the molecular identity matches the compound named on the label.

Method and date

Which analysis was run, and when, so you can judge how current the figure is.
